# Freela · Wise × Sentry
References: design/references/wise.md and design/references/sentry.md.

React Native app. Wise's calm transactional hierarchy and green actions meet Sentry's violet identity and Rubik typography.
Palette: ink #1F1633, green #9FE870, canvas #F5F6F2, white #FFFFFF, lavender #EEE9F6, muted #665F73.
Rubik regular for text, medium for controls, bold for titles. Large numbers for amounts and price.
Left-aligned content. 24px screen padding. Divided lists for work. Rounded 24px hero for the main decision.
Mobile: header → summary → action → work list → bottom navigation. Content capped at 1080px, forms at 560px.
Freela+: violet feature area, green action, monthly terms, restore. Free: 5 professional quotes/calendar month in America/Sao_Paulo. Client requests stay separate.
Explicit light theme; keyboard-aware forms; safe areas; visible labels; 48px controls; in-flight locks; recoverable quote drafts.
Review: green follows both references; violet is reserved for identity and Freela+. No glass, neon glows, floating decoration, web-only layout or proprietary fonts.
